# Getting Started with XRAI

XRAI provides real-time speech-to-text subtitles, translation, and text-to-speech — on your phone, tablet, or AR glasses.

## First Launch & Onboarding

When you first open XRAI, you'll be guided through a setup flow:

1. **Welcome** - Accept Terms of Service and Privacy Policy
2. **Language** - Choose your primary language and optional translation target language
3. **Region** - Select your geographic region
4. **Permissions** - Grant microphone access (required) and camera access (optional, for AR)
5. **Smart Glasses** (optional) - Pair AR glasses if you have them
6. **Overview** - Quick tour of key features

## Choosing a Mode

After onboarding, the Home Screen lets you choose between two modes:

### Basic Mode

A simplified interface designed for quick, straightforward subtitling. Features include:

- Large, easy-to-read subtitles
- Simple font size controls (S / M / L)
- Quick theme selection
- Single language pair
- Minimal settings

### Enhanced / AR Mode

The full-featured interface with access to all settings and capabilities:

- Split-screen translation
- Full engine priority control
- Advanced typography and color settings
- AR glasses support
- Multi-channel audio (Dante)
- Speaker identification

## Starting a Session

1. Tap the **+** button or the microphone button to begin a new subtitle session
2. XRAI will start listening and displaying subtitles in real time
3. Use the bottom toolbar to access settings, change languages, or manage engines

![Active Subtitle Session](../screenshots/subtitle-session.png)

## The Bottom Toolbar

The toolbar at the bottom of the screen provides quick access to key features:

![Bottom Toolbar](../screenshots/bottom-toolbar.png)

- **Language icon** - Open the language drawer to change languages
- **Mode icon** - Short tap to cycle processing modes (Cloud/On Device/Custom); long press to open the full AI Engine Priority panel
- **Microphone / Stop** - Start or stop a session
- **Free / Pro badge** - View your usage and subscription status
- **Settings icon** - Open the settings drawer

## Stopping a Session

Tap the **Stop** button in the toolbar. You'll be asked to:

- **Keep** - Save the transcript to your conversation history
- **Discard** - Delete the session without saving
- **Cancel** - Continue the session

![Stop Session Dialog](../screenshots/stop-session-dialog.png)
