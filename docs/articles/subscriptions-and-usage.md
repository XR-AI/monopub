# Subscriptions & Usage

XRAI offers multiple subscription tiers to match your needs, from free basic transcription to full-featured professional use.

## Subscription Tiers

### Free (Essentials)

- Cloud speech-to-text transcription
- Basic features and settings
- Limited engine selection

### Premium

- Everything in Free
- On-device models for offline use
- Offline speech-to-text and translation
- Text-to-Speech capability
- Expanded engine selection
- Speaker identification

### Ultimate

- Everything in Premium
- All cloud engines at priority tier
- Advanced engine priority customization
- Full access to all features

## Pro Minutes

Cloud-based AI engines consume **Pro Minutes** from your subscription balance. Different engines have different consumption rates:

- **Free engines** (green badge) - No Pro Minutes consumed
- **Metered engines** (x1 badge) - Consume Pro Minutes based on usage duration

### Viewing Your Usage

![Consumption Dialog](../screenshots/consumption-dialog.png)

Tap the **Free** or **Pro** badge in the bottom toolbar to open the Consumption Dialog. This shows:

- Remaining Pro Minutes
- Breakdown by category (STT, Translation, TTS)
- Per-minute rate for each active engine
- Total consumption rate multiplier

### Reducing Usage

To reduce Pro Minutes consumption:

- Switch to **On Device** mode for free on-device processing
- Use **Data Saver** mode to prefer free engines
- Select free engines (marked with green "Free" badge) in the Engine Priority settings

## Managing Your Subscription

Access subscription management from:

- **Profile** > **Subscription**
- The upsell screen (appears when subscription limits are reached)

## Session Limits

Some subscription tiers have per-session duration limits. When the limit is reached, you'll be prompted to:

- Start a new session
- Upgrade your subscription for longer sessions

## Feature Availability

Features marked with a lock icon require a higher subscription tier. Tap the lock to see which plan unlocks the feature.
