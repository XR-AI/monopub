# AR Glasses & Device Pairing

XRAI supports AR smart glasses for an immersive subtitle experience projected directly into your field of view.

## Supported Devices

XRAI works with compatible AR glasses that support the XRAI companion app. Check the XRAI website for the latest list of supported devices.

## Pairing Your Glasses

### During Onboarding

The Smart Glasses setup screen appears during first-launch onboarding. Follow the on-screen instructions to pair your glasses.

### After Onboarding

1. Tap the **pairing icon** in the toolbar or go to **Settings > Devices**
2. The pairing drawer shows a local pairing code
3. Enter this code on your AR glasses or scan the QR code
4. Wait for the connection to be established

![Pairing Drawer](../screenshots/pairing-drawer.png)

### Pairing Status

Devices show one of three states:
- **Paired** - Connected and ready
- **Pending** - Connection in progress
- **Unpaired** - Not connected

## AR Display Settings

When glasses are connected, additional settings become available:

### Font Size

Choose from 24px, 28px, or 32px text size on the glasses display.

### Text Position

Position subtitles at:
- **Bottom** - Lower portion of the display
- **Top** - Upper portion of the display
- **Center** - Middle of the display

### Subtitle Distance (3DoF)

On glasses with 3 degrees of freedom tracking, adjust the perceived distance of subtitles from 0.5m to 16m.

### Brightness

Adjust the glasses display brightness from 0 (dimmest) to 5 (brightest).

### Video Passthrough

Toggle real-world video in the background behind subtitles.

## Audio Modes

AR glasses support specialized audio recording modes:

- **Face-to-face** - Directional microphone optimized for the person in front of you
- **360 with Noise Cancellation** - Captures all directions with background noise filtering
- **360 without Noise Cancellation** - Captures all directions without filtering

### Hide Wearer's Voice

Toggle this option to exclude the glasses wearer's own voice from the transcript, useful when you only want to caption what others are saying.

## Managing Devices

### View Paired Devices

Open the device drawer to see all currently paired devices and their connection status.

### Unpair a Device

Select a paired device and choose **Unpair** to disconnect it.

## Enhanced vs Basic Mode

- **Enhanced Mode** - Full AR experience with glasses display, spatial audio, and all advanced features
- **Basic Mode** - Phone/tablet only, simplified interface without AR features

Select your mode from the Home Screen before starting a session.
