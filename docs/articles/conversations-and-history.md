# Conversations & History

XRAI saves your subtitle sessions as conversations that you can review, search, and export later.

## Viewing Conversations

![Conversations List](../screenshots/conversations-list.png)

The Conversations screen shows all your saved sessions in a list. Each conversation displays:

- **Session title** (auto-generated or custom)
- **Date** of the session
- **Preview** of the transcript text
- **Speaker badge** showing the speaker configuration used

Tap a conversation to open it and review the full transcript.

![Conversation Detail](../screenshots/conversation-detail.png)

## Searching Conversations

Use the search bar at the top of the Conversations screen to find specific sessions by:

- Speaker name
- Language used
- Transcript content (full-text search)

## Managing Conversations

### Rename

Tap and hold a conversation to rename it with a custom title.

### Delete

Swipe or use the menu to delete a conversation permanently. This action cannot be undone.

### Export

Export transcripts in multiple formats:

- **Text** - Plain text transcript
- **Captions** - Subtitle file format (SRT/VTT)
- **Audio** - Audio recording of the session (if available)
- **ZIP** - All formats bundled together

## Session Details

Each conversation stores metadata including:

- Duration of the session
- Languages used (source and target)
- Speakers identified
- Engine used for transcription

## Starting a New Conversation

Tap the **+** button on the Conversations screen or the microphone button in the toolbar to begin a new subtitle session. When you stop the session, you'll be asked whether to Keep or Discard the transcript.
